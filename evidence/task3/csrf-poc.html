CSRF proof-of-concept (localhost teaching demo)

If you are logged into the app at http://127.0.0.1:3000, this page will submit a profile change on your behalf with no CSRF token.

Against the hardened build this fails. You will land on /login and your display name will be unchanged — because SameSite=Lax stopped the browser from sending your session cookie at all, so the request arrived anonymous. Expect a 303, not a 403: a browser cannot reach the token check. Run node evidence/task3/capture-csrf-layers.mjs to see the other two layers.
